PVGames permitted-use record
=============================

This project retains the relevant permitted-use terms supplied with the locally
owned PVGames packs used as environmental reference material:

- The resources may be used in commercial and non-commercial projects.
- The resources may be edited or otherwise transformed for use in a project.
- Raw resources and edited resource files may not be redistributed as standalone
  assets or resource packs.
- Attribution and the originating source pack must be retained with each
  transformed output.

Task 5 compliance
-----------------

The Sea Bomber outputs recorded in painted-scenery-sources.json are original,
repainted 256x256 material swatches. They retain no complete source building or
arch and are used only by scenery and terrain painters. Source archives and raw
source objects are not included in this repository or production build.
